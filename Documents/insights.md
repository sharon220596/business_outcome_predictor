From the project:

📌 High churn segments
	•	Low tenure customers
	•	Customers with high monthly charges
	•	Month-to-month contract users
	•	Customers using Fiber optic internet

📌 Why customers churn
	1.	High monthly cost vs perceived value
	2.	Short relationship with company (tenure)
	3.	Contract cancellations are easy
	4.	High competition among telecom providers

📌 What the business should do
	•	Provide loyalty offers for new users
	•	Reduce price shocks for high-charge users
	•	Encourage annual contracts
	•	Improve customer service quality
	•	Build proactive retention campaigns

conclusion:This project provides a complete solution to predict and analyze telecom customer churn.
