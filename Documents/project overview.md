📌 1. Project Overview

The Business Outcome Predictor is an end-to-end machine learning pipeline designed to predict customer churn for a telecom company.

The goal is to allow the business to:
	•	Identify customers at risk of leaving
	•	Take action early (discounts, offers, support)
	•	Reduce revenue loss & increase customer loyalty

This system processes raw data → cleans it → analyzes it → builds ML models → evaluates → generates insights → produces figures & model files.
